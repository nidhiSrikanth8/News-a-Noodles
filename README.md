# News-a-Noodles - Your Delicious Source for Latest News

Welcome to **News-a-Noodles**, your one-stop place for the latest news from around the world! Just like a bowl of noodles, we serve you news hot, fresh, and full of flavor. Whether you're in the mood for global events, breaking news, or detailed articles, News-a-Noodles has it all!

## Features

- **Latest News Updates**: Always stay up-to-date with the freshest news stories.
- **Full-Length Articles**: Dive deep into articles and enjoy comprehensive news coverage.
- **Intuitive Design**: Simple, clean, and easy-to-use interface to browse through the news.
- **Clickable Thumbnails**: Click on any thumbnail to read the full article and explore more.

## Technologies Used

- **Node.js**: Backend runtime environment to handle the server-side logic.
- **Express.js**: Web framework to manage routes and serve views.
- **EJS**: Templating engine for rendering dynamic views (news list and article page).
- **CSS**: Styling the web pages with modern and responsive design.
- **HTML**: Structuring the content for the news and article pages.
